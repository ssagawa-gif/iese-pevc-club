# Catalogue of the club library

What the IESE PE/VC Club actually holds, by module, so a student can be pointed to the original document. The files themselves are **not** bundled with this plugin — they are third-party material and live in the club's shared folder. Ask the club for access.

Where a file name appears twice with the same content, it is listed once and the duplicate noted.

## VC Resources

### 1. Intro to VC
- Intro to VC (Avidan Rudansky) — fund structure, personnel, 2-and-20, fund mechanics. *Also filed as "Introduction To Venture Capital".*
- How VC Works (Bob Zider, HBR) — what venture money actually funds, and why the asset class exists
- Venture Capital 101 (Brian Rothenberg) — the five things VCs do, stage ladder, what they look for
- Basics of Venture Capital Financing (Jeff Bussgang, Flybridge / HBS, 2021) — round sizes and prices, milestones, VCs vs angels, when VC is the wrong instrument
- How venture capital works — the math behind VC (Guhesh Ramanathan) — fund arithmetic and the 5 Ts. *Also filed under Career & Industry Insights as "Understanding how VCs think".*
- The Ultimate VC Metrics Cheat Sheet (Diligent Equity) — MOIC, TVPI, RVPI, DPI, gross vs net IRR
- Venture Capital and the Finance of Innovation
- VC Journey
- Venture Capital Dictionary

### 2. Sourcing & Deal Flow
- Deal Sourcing, Analysis and Due Diligence (Les Alexander, Darden) — the process end to end, HBR data on channels and conversion. *Also filed in module 3 as "Deal Sourcing & Due Diligence".*
- Strategic Deal Sourcing for Family Offices

### 3. Due Diligence & Investment Process
- Venture Capital Due Diligence (Robert Carroll, Oasis500) — the three stages: screening, business, legal
- GCV Investor Due Diligence Checklist — team, business, market, financials, legal
- DD Questionnaire
- Due Diligence Guide
- The Venture Capital Due Diligence Questionnaire — *near-duplicate of "VC - DD Questionnaire" and "Venture Capital Due Diligence Questionnaire"*
- VC DD Questionnaire
- Mistakes VCs make during DD (image)
- SIMPLE Framework — DD (image)

### 4. Valuations
- Aswath Damodaran — Venture Capital Valuation Method
- Valuing Pre-Revenue Startups (eVenturing collection, incl. Villalobos on divergence, a valuation worksheet and estimator)
- Fundamental Startup Metrics (Linas Beliūnas) — TAM, ARR, GMV, ARPU, churn, LTV, CAC, burn multiple, NPS, concentration
- Pre-Money Valuation
- Pre-money vs Post-money — Valuation Guide
- Valuation Guidelines
- How to Talk About Valuation Numbers
- VC Valuation Method Model (.xlsx) — the only spreadsheet in the library

### 5. Term Sheets & Investment Memos
- Preparing a VC Term Sheet (Morgan, Lewis & Bockius) — economics, control, protections, with a sample term sheet. *Also filed as "Venture Capital Term Sheet".*
- VC Term Sheet Guide — HSBC
- VC TERMSHEET — SVB UK
- Investment Memo Template — Y Combinator

### 6. Capital Structuring
- Cap Tables: Formation to Exit (Capdesk) — UK-centred, the option pool and convertible note shuffles. *Filed twice.*
- SAFEs vs. Convertible Notes vs. Priced Equity (Dror Futter, 2024) — with Carta pre-seed market data

### 7. Post-Investment — Value Creation & Portfolio Management
- Fund Operations: Portfolio Management & Construction — conviction vs diversification, monitoring by stage
- Value Creation 2.0 — INSEAD

### 8. Exit Strategies & Secondary Markets
- The Path to Liquidity in Venture Capital (AVP) — years to 1.0x DPI, secondaries market data
- Stages of VC Financing (image)

## Known gaps

The library has no worked LBO model, no PE diligence templates, no current market data (the newest documents are 2024), and little on Europe or Latin America specifically. When a question lands in one of those gaps, say so and search rather than stretching the material.
