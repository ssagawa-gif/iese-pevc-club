# PE/VC Club Toolkit

Tools built by the IESE PE/VC Club for Claude.

## Investment Thesis Coach

A coach, not a generator. You do the thinking; the skill asks the question you would rather not answer.

**How to use it.** Once installed, say something like:

- "I want to build an investment thesis on last-mile logistics in Brazil."
- "Here's my thesis on European defence tech — tear it apart."
- "Help me prepare a category thesis for a VC interview."

**What happens.** The skill frames the session (build from scratch, or audit a thesis you already wrote), then walks through 14 blocks, one at a time. At each block it asks, listens, stresses your answer from at least two angles, sources anything factual, scores it 1–5, and only opens the next block at 3 or above.

**The three locks that make it worth using:**

1. **Zero invention.** Every number carries publisher, title, URL and the vintage of the data. Anything it cannot find becomes a visible `[LACUNA: ...]` in the final document. An honest gap beats a plausible wrong number.
2. **The score gate.** Below a 3, the block does not pass — the skill reframes the question from a different angle. After two failed attempts it gives one worked example from a *different* sector, never yours.
3. **It will not write it for you.** "Just write the thesis" gets refused once, with the reason, and handed back as Block 1. The final assembly uses only the answers you validated yourself.

**Language.** The coach mirrors you — Portuguese, Spanish or English — while keeping VC terminology in English (*why now*, *moat*, *kill criteria*, *venture-scale*), which is how you will be interviewed.

**Output.** A published page with six required visuals (why-now timeline, market map, bottom-up TAM build, moat diagram, consensus vs. thesis, kill-criteria tracker) and a memo version under 400 words.

## The 14 blocks

1. Observation · 2. Structural driver · 3. Why now · 4. Market consequence · 5. Behavior change · 6. Business model · 7. Winner characteristics · 8. Defensibility · 9. Contrarian belief · 10. Risks / disconfirming evidence · 11. Investable companies · 12. Timing · 13. Return potential · 14. Kill criteria

## Course library

The skill reserves a **Course library** section for the frameworks, cases and rubric from Prof. Thomas. While it is empty, the coach runs on web sources alone. Filling it is the largest quality gain available — it is what makes the skill teach the course's method rather than a generic one.
