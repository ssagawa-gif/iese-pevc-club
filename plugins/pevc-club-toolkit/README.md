# PE/VC Club Toolkit

Tools built by the IESE PE/VC Club for Claude. Two skills.

---

## 1. Investment Thesis Coach

A coach, not a generator. You do the thinking; the skill asks the question you would rather not answer.

**How to use it.** Once installed, say something like:

- "I want to build an investment thesis on last-mile logistics in Brazil."
- "Here's my thesis on European defence tech — tear it apart."
- "Help me prepare a category thesis for a VC interview."

**What happens.** The skill frames the session (build from scratch, or audit a thesis you already wrote), then walks through 14 blocks, one at a time. At each block it asks, listens, stresses your answer from at least two angles, sources anything factual, scores it 1–5, and only opens the next block at 3 or above.

**The locks that make it worth using:**

1. **Zero invention.** Every number carries publisher, title, URL and the vintage of the data. Anything it cannot find becomes a visible `[LACUNA: ...]` in the final document. An honest gap beats a plausible wrong number.
2. **The score gate.** Below a 3, the block does not pass — the skill reframes the question from a different angle. After two failed attempts it gives one worked example from a *different* sector, never yours.
3. **It will not write it for you.** "Just write the thesis" gets refused once, with the reason, and handed back as Block 1.
4. **It writes so you can read it.** One idea per sentence, short paragraphs, concrete nouns and real numbers, VC terms kept but explained once. The published page opens with a summary in plain words — no jargon at all.

**The 14 blocks.** Observation · structural driver · why now · market consequence · behavior change · business model · winner characteristics · defensibility · contrarian belief · risks and disconfirming evidence · investable companies · timing · return potential · kill criteria.

**Language.** The coach mirrors you — Portuguese, Spanish or English — while keeping VC terminology in English (*why now*, *moat*, *kill criteria*, *venture-scale*), which is how you will be interviewed.

**Output.** A published page with six required visuals (why-now timeline, market map, bottom-up TAM build, moat diagram, consensus vs. thesis, kill-criteria tracker) and a memo version under 400 words.

## 2. PE/VC Library

The club's teaching material, distilled into a reference desk. Ask it anything the course covers:

- "What's the difference between a SAFE and a convertible note?"
- "Explain the option pool shuffle."
- "What's a good burn multiple?"
- "How long does a VC fund take to return capital?"
- "What should I read before a VC interview?"

It answers from the club's own material first, names the source and its vintage, and points you to the original document when you should read it yourself. For building a thesis it hands off to the coach rather than half-doing the job.

Covers: VC fundamentals · sourcing and due diligence · valuation and metrics · term sheets and investment memos · cap tables and early-stage instruments · portfolio construction and exits · private equity and buy-outs · careers and interviews, plus a catalogue of the club's files.

---

## About the source material

The club's PDF library is third-party material and is **not** bundled with this plugin. What ships here are original summaries with attribution, plus a catalogue so students can open the originals from the club's shared folder.

Benchmark figures carry their source and vintage. They are reference points, not current prices — both skills are instructed to search for a current figure before putting a market number in any document.
